01 — ABOUT

Engineering with a
hands-on bias.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware and software. I enjoy taking a physical system, understanding the signals and constraints around it, and building the software that makes the system easier to control, measure, and use.

At SIUE, that has included LabVIEW-based instrumentation, embedded programming, digital systems, machine learning, circuit analysis, and C++ game development. Outside the lab, I lead and compete with SIUE Club Tennis and spend plenty of time traveling.

Hero Run

An embedded C++ game inspired by the Chrome Dino runner, built around a character LCD and push-button input. The original program uses custom LCD sprites, terrain generation, interrupt-driven input, collision detection, and score progression.
